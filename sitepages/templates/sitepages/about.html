About Atlas Cargo Systems

A clearing, forwarding and logistics service provider operating in Uganda since 1995

Who we are

A client-centric clearing and forwarding company

Atlas Cargo Systems is a client-centric clearing and forwarding company, ardent about serving our clients with excellence in meeting their unique needs. We meet the requirements of our clients end-to-end — source to destination — across the logistics and clearing value chain.

We support our clientele through every step of the forwarding supply chain, from consulting and concept development through planning, management, control and execution. The management of consignment, warehousing, processing, deployment, transportation, tracking and storage is handled seamlessly, so our services stay bespoke to each client's needs — rendered with diligence and passion.

Our vision

To evolve into the region's world-class freight forwarding and logistics company.

Our mission

We seek to become the preferred provider of holistic freight forwarding and logistics services. To accomplish this, we pledge to:

  • Deliver higher quality service to our clients
  • Customize our service to suit our clients' needs
  • Communicate and deal with our clients with the utmost honesty and transparency
  • Be receptive to the concerns and needs of our clients
  • Continuously build the capacity of our staff to enhance service delivery through use of technology
  • Stay one step ahead of our clients by anticipating their needs

How we work

Our approach

We embed a multi-modal approach in our operations so that our clients can stay informed about every process. The continuous flow of information between our operatives and our clients ensures continuity in our clients' business processes.

We also integrate our freight forwarding functions so that the outcome is driven by the business forecast, based on the strategic objectives of Atlas Cargo Systems — converging the right experts and extending planning to our clients' logistics assets.

Recognized by our industry

Affiliations, certifications & awards

UFFA member

Uganda Freight Forwarders Association — the umbrella body for logistics companies in Uganda, whose members handle over 90% of the country's import and export freight.

FIATA affiliated

The International Federation of Freight Forwarders Associations — the global voice of freight logistics, representing forwarding and logistics firms in 150 countries.

AEO certified

Authorised Economic Operator status with Uganda Revenue Authority — a trade facilitation program rewarding customs compliance with fast clearance and reduced inspection.
