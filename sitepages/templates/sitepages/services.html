Our Services

Supply chain solutions from source to final destination

Atlas Cargo Systems furnishes supply chain solutions from source to final destination. Our services encompass clearance, freight forwarding, warehousing, transportation, port services, cargo handling and other managed logistics services — integrated, or standing individually, to suit clients large and small.

Air freight forwarding

Atlas Cargo Systems offers international and domestic airfreight forwarding services connecting all major global markets, meeting your global transportation needs within Uganda and around the world — from small to large consignments, handled with integrity and professionalism.

  • Door-to-door and airport-to-airport service and pricing options
  • Charters and special cargo
  • Consolidation and direct-to-consignees service

Ocean freight forwarding

Our credible working relations with shipping lines give clients access to a global carrier portfolio spanning major ports, with the best transit times, flexible scheduling and space protection agreements to meet your individual shipping requirements.

  • Domestic and international ocean freight, worldwide consolidation
  • LCL (Less than Container Load) and FCL (Full Container Load)
  • Ocean charter and container management

Road freight forwarding

From small trucks to prime movers, our diverse fleet provides road freight transport for everything from parcels and documents to oversized machinery and bulk commodities — with transport management and planning that responds quickly to disruptions such as weather, additional pick-ups or heavy traffic.

Warehousing

Atlas provides a holistic warehousing and distribution package, managed by a dedicated team, with an advanced network and warehouse planning that leads to continuous improvement across the Atlas affiliate warehouse grid.

  • Warehouse storage and bonded facilities
  • Crafting, hazardous cargo, FCL and break-bulk transport
  • Container packing & unpacking, tracking and tracing, stock inventory and status reporting

Country-wide logistics

Speedy, efficient and low-cost delivery of all types of cargo locally, including temperature-sensitive and delicate shipments. Our experience in bio-pharma transportation is among the best in the market — organizations including Joint Medical Stores, Medical Access and the Ministry of Health have relied on our countrywide distribution, upcountry included, cold-chain shipments handled within the pharmaceutical industry's standard 2–8°C tolerance.

Technology

We operate in a complex international sector where efficiency to international standard is crucial. Our IT systems are built to handle the functions a fully automated logistics process needs:

  • Manifesting plus integrated load & yard management
  • Inbound transportation management and freight auditing
  • Electronic data interchange (EDI), inbound and outbound tracking
  • Transportation procurement, planning and routing guide management

Customs brokerage

Our technical knowledge of customs service gives us a unique comprehension of the laws and regulations, licenses and procedures, concessions and tariffs, valuations and trade agreements across Uganda and the region. Our experienced, licensed customs agents handle documentation for import and export, and advise on trade, tax rates and duty exemptions.

Cargo crisis management

When cargo handling standards fall out of step with the level, timing or area a shipment demands, prompt resolution matters — a company's reputation and its clients' business opportunities are at stake. Atlas provides prompt support and counsel in logistics management, ready to assist clients in crisis situations seven days a week with the best available forwarding solutions.
